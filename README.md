# AudioCycle

### Your audio. Your format. No ads. No in-app purchases.

AudioCycle is an audio converter in development with a simple goal: make it easy to convert audio files and extract audio from video files into a wide range of useful audio formats.

The aim is broad compatibility, supporting as many input formats as practical and producing audio that plays in VLC, at a minimum.

## Project Status

AudioCycle is in early development. Features, supported formats, and platform availability are still being worked out.

This README describes the direction of the project, not a guarantee that every feature is available yet.

## Why AudioCycle?

Converting audio should be straightforward, without advertisements or in-app purchases getting in the way.

I’m new to app development, and I’m building AudioCycle to learn while creating something useful. My goal is an approachable converter that helps people get their audio into the format they need.

## Project Goals

- Convert between a broad range of audio formats.
- Extract audio from video files.
- Produce files that are playable in VLC and, where possible, other common media players.
- Keep the conversion process simple and easy to understand.
- Remain completely free of advertisements and in-app purchases.

## Planned Workflow

1. Choose an audio or video file.
2. Select an audio output format.
3. Adjust any available conversion settings.
4. Choose where to save the result.
5. Convert.

The interface and available settings may change as development progresses.

## Format Support

The goal is wide format support, not a promise that every file can be converted into every format.

A confirmed list of supported input and output formats will be added as they are implemented and tested.

## Installation

Installation instructions and supported platforms will be added when a testable build is available.

## Feedback and Bug Reports

Feedback is welcome, especially while the app is taking shape.

When reporting a conversion problem, please include:

- Your operating system and AudioCycle version.
- The original file’s format or extension.
- The output format you selected.
- Any error message you received.
- The steps needed to reproduce the issue.

Please only share sample files that you have permission to distribute.

## Ads and In-App Purchases

AudioCycle is being built to be completely ad-free and free of in-app purchases.

No ads. No conversion-credit purchases. Just a focus on making a useful audio converter.
